How to play

01

A moth circles you

Its light casts your shadow across the islands.

02

Tap anywhere

You jump to the tip of your shadow.

03

Read the shadow

Black: safe landing. Red: the void, a wall or crystals.

04

Don't wait too long

Islands crumble beneath you. A low moth makes a long, fast shadow.
