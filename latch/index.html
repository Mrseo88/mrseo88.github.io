LATCH

One tap. Infinite rage. Let go, fly, latch on.

You are a spark orbiting a node. Tap to let go and fly straight, tap again to latch onto the nearest node. Your new orbit is exactly as tight as your timing: hug the core for a PERFECT, but tight orbits spin faster, and every node burns out under you.

Get it on Google Play Play in your browser

How to play

01

Tap to let go

You leave the orbit and fly in a straight line along the tangent.

02

Tap to latch

You grab the nearest node. Your distance at that instant becomes your new orbit.

03

Hug the core

Latch close for a PERFECT. Chain them for combos up to x8, skip a node for double points.

04

Don't stay

Every node burns out under you. Walls, cores and red spikes end the run.
