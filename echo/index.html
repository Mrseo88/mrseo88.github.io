ECHOLightrunner

One tap. Total dark. Every tap lights the way.

You are a dot of light in a zig-zag corridor. Each tap turns you and sends out a pulse that shows the walls for a moment. You need the light to see, but every time you ask for it, you have to turn.

How to play

01

Tap to turn

Every tap switches your direction in the corridor.

02

Every tap is light

A pulse shows the walls for a moment, then the dark closes in.

03

Hit the corner

Tap right on the turn for a Perfect. Chain them and your light grows.

04

Don't touch the walls

The corridor gets faster and narrower. One touch and it's lights out.
