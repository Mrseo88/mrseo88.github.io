Raft Brawl

Knock them in. Don't fall in.

A round little wrestler on a wooden raft, with water all around. The arrow spins around you: hold to plant your feet and charge, release to dash where it points. Send everyone flying into the water, wave after wave, without taking the plunge yourself.

Get it on Google Play Play in your browser

How to play

1

Hold to charge

The arrow stops and you plant your feet: you get heavier and harder to push around.

2

Release to dash

You dash where the arrow points. Release when the ring turns gold for a PERFECT hit.

3

Knock them in

Every enemy in the water scores. Chain dunks for combos, caroms and FEVER.

4

Don't fall in

The dotted line shows where you'll stop. If it turns red, you're going swimming.
