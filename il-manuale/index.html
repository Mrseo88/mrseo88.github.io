Il Manuale del Gioco

The manual lies. Rewrite it.

GRAND ADVENTURE DELUXE was meant to be the most awaited game of 1996. It never came out. All that's left is its instruction booklet, and whatever is written in it comes true. A missing button, an erased bridge, a door that won't open: the answer is always somewhere in the pages.

3

4

Think, don't guess
