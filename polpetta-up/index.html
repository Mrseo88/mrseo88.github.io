The impossible climb

Hold to stretch. Let go to grab.

You're a meatball with a noodle for an arm. Hold your finger down and the arm stretches toward it; let go over the next meatball and you snap up to it. Hold too long and you sail right past. Below you, the Primordial Ragù keeps rising.

How to play

1

Hold

Your arm stretches toward your finger. The longer you hold, the farther it reaches.

2

Let go

Release with the hand over a meatball to snap up to it. Overshoot and you miss.

3

Mind the arm

Pizza cutters, flying slippers and espresso beams can cut a stretched arm in half.

4

Keep moving

The ragù never stops rising. Falling? Stretch again and grab something on the way down.
